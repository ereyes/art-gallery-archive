About the Gallery

In-Betweens

The SIGGRAPH 2026 Art Gallery

The SIGGRAPH 2026 Art Gallery explored computer graphics and interactive techniques as languages and materials for expressing artistic visions and shaping aesthetic experiences.

Under the theme “In-Betweens,” fourteen installations foregrounded relationships across space, time, technology, and generations. The exhibition brought together screens, projections, interactive installations, sculptures, retro devices, wearables, bio art, data visualization, robotics, and space art.

Published Preface

In-Betweens

The published preface extends this brief introduction into a guided reading of the twelve juried installations and two curated contributions. It follows the exhibition theme across bodies, computation, memory, biology, and planetary time.

It also situates the gallery within SIGGRAPH’s longer history of computer art—from the 1981 Frame Buffer Show to contemporary animation, AI, and real-time systems.
